# NDRF & MoEFCC Chemical Hazard Emergency & Shelter-In-Place Protocol (2024)
**Document ID:** NDRF-CBRN-CHEM-2024-V1
**Source Agency:** National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) / Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC)
**Disaster Category:** Chemical Spill / Hazardous Toxic Vapor Release / Industrial Chemical Hazard
**Applicable Environment:** Industrial corridors, highway transport spill zones, chemical refinery perimeters
**Last Updated:** 2024-04-18

---

### Section 1: Shelter-in-Place Directives
1. **Immediate Indoor Sheltering:** In the event of a toxic chemical vapor cloud release, immediately enter the nearest substantial building. Go to an interior room above ground level, as many hazardous chemical gases (such as chlorine and ammonia) are heavier than air and settle in basements and low-lying ground.
2. **HVAC & Ventilation Shutoff:** Immediately turn off all air conditioners, exhaust fans, heating ventilation systems, and fireplace dampers. Ensure all air exchange with the outdoors is stopped.
3. **Seal Doors & Windows:** Seal gaps around windows, doors, and air vents using plastic sheeting, duct tape, or damp towels.

---

### Section 2: Outdoor Evacuation & Windward Movement
1. **Move Crosswind / Upwind:** If outdoors during a toxic vapor release, determine the wind direction immediately. Evacuate **crosswind** (perpendicular to the wind direction) or **upwind** (away from where the smoke/vapor cloud is moving). Never run downwind with the plume.
2. **Respiratory Protection:** Cover your nose and mouth with a damp cloth, handkerchief, or certified chemical respirator mask. Breathe shallowly and avoid talking.

---

### Section 3: Decontamination & Eye Washing
1. **Flush Eyes & Skin:** If exposed to chemical mist or vapor, flush eyes with clean running water for at least 15 minutes. Remove contaminated outer clothing immediately and wash skin with mild soap and copious water.
2. **Emergency Numbers:** Chemical Disaster Cell / NDRF **1077**, Fire Service **101**, National Emergency **112**, Poison Control **1800-116-117**.
