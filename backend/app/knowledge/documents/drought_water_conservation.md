# NDMA & Ministry of Jal Shakti Drought Management, Water Conservation & Agricultural Advisory (2024)
**Document ID:** NDMA-MJS-DROUGHT-2024-V1
**Source Agency:** National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) / Ministry of Jal Shakti / IMD
**Disaster Category:** Drought / Extreme Aridity / Water Scarcity Emergency
**Applicable Environment:** Semi-arid agricultural zones, rain-shadow river basins, depleted groundwater districts
**Last Updated:** 2024-05-25

---

### Section 1: Potable Water Rationing & Conservation Protocols
1. **Prioritize Drinking Water:** In severe drought declared sectors, strictly prioritize municipal water reserves for human drinking, cooking, and livestock sustenance. Prohibit non-essential potable water usage including vehicle washing, lawn irrigation, and decorative fountains.
2. **Safe Water Storage & Chlorination:** Store potable water in sealed, food-grade containers away from direct sunlight. Treat stored ground or tanker water with chlorine tablets (0.5 mg/L free residual chlorine) to prevent microbial proliferation during prolonged storage.
3. **Emergency Water Tanker Points:** Collect water only from officially certified municipal water distribution tankers or designated deep tube-wells monitored for total dissolved solids (TDS) and salinity.

---

### Section 2: Crop Protection, Livestock Care & Heat Stresses
1. **Livestock Fodder & Shade:** Relocate farm cattle and livestock to shaded community fodder camps. Ensure cattle receive at least 35 to 50 liters of clean drinking water per day supplemented with mineral salt licks to prevent fatal heat dehydration.
2. **Micro-Irrigation & Mulching:** Transition agricultural fields to drip or sprinkler micro-irrigation systems during morning or late evening hours to reduce evaporative loss by up to 60%. Apply organic straw mulching across topsoil to retain root-zone moisture.
3. **Emergency Helplines:** Central Drought Management Cell **1070**, District Water Crisis Helpline **1077**, Kisan Emergency Helpline **1800-180-1551**.
