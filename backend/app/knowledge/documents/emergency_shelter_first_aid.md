# NDMA & Red Cross Emergency Shelter Procedures & Family Preparedness Guidelines (2024)
**Document ID:** NDMA-RC-PREP-2024-V2
**Source Agency:** National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) / Indian Red Cross Society (IRCS) / WHO
**Disaster Category:** Emergency Preparedness / Emergency Shelter Management / First Aid & Family Safety
**Applicable Environment:** Multi-hazard community shelters, urban relief camps, family households
**Last Updated:** 2024-05-10

---

### Section 1: Emergency Shelter Arrival & Registration
1. **Shelter Registration:** Upon arriving at an official emergency shelter, register all family members immediately with the shelter administration desk. This enables authorities to maintain accurate headcounts and reunite separated family members.
2. **Special Needs & Medical Needs:** Notify shelter medical coordinators immediately of any pregnant family members, infants, elderly dependents, individuals requiring daily insulin/dialysis, or oxygen-dependent patients.
3. **Hygiene & Sanitation:** Adhere strictly to designated sanitation zones, wash hands frequently with soap or sanitizer, and report any symptoms of fever, diarrhea, or rash immediately to shelter medical staff.

---

### Section 2: Family Go-Bag & Survival Supplies
1. **3-Day Emergency Supplies Checklist:**
   - **Water:** 3 liters of potable drinking water per person per day.
   - **Food:** Non-perishable, ready-to-eat dry rations (biscuits, energy bars, roasted gram, canned food).
   - **First Aid Kit:** Bandages, sterile gauze pads, antiseptic lotion (Betadine), burn ointment, ORS packets, paracetamol, and a 14-day supply of personal prescription medications.
   - **Tools & Power:** LED flashlight with spare batteries, power bank with charging cables, emergency whistle for rescue signaling, and battery-powered portable AM/FM radio.
   - **Sanitation:** Wet wipes, soap, toothbrushes, sanitary pads, chlorine water purification tablets, and heavy-duty garbage bags.

---

### Section 3: Official Verification & Anti-Rumor Advisory
1. **Rely Exclusively on Official Channels:** Only act on alerts and directives broadcast by the District Magistrate / Collector, State Disaster Management Authority (SDMA), NDRF, SDRF, or Police Control Rooms.
2. **Do Not Forward Unverified Social Media Rumors:** During disasters, false claims regarding dam breaches, fake death tolls, or non-existent relief centers cause life-threatening panic. Cross-verify suspicious messages with the official Disaster Control Room before sharing.
3. **Emergency Numbers:** National Emergency Number **112**, State Disaster Helpline **1070**, District Disaster Helpline **1077**, Ambulance **108**.
