# IMD & NDMA Cyclone Preparedness, High-Wind Safety & Storm Surge Guidelines (2024)
**Document ID:** IMD-NDMA-CYC-2024-V1
**Source Agency:** India Meteorological Department (IMD) / National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA)
**Disaster Category:** Cyclone / Severe Cyclonic Storm / Coastal Storm Surge
**Applicable Environment:** Coastal districts, maritime zones, high-wind vulnerability belts
**Last Updated:** 2024-05-20

---

### Section 1: Cyclone Alert Phases & Coastal Evacuation
1. **Mandatory Coastal Evacuation:** When a Cyclone Warning (Orange/Red Alert) is issued with expected landfall within 24 to 36 hours, residents within 5 km of the coastline or low-lying coastal estuaries must evacuate to fortified Multi-Purpose Cyclone Shelters (MPCS) or designated pucca buildings.
2. **Fishermen & Maritime Ban:** All fishing operations, coastal boating, and maritime port activities must cease immediately. Fishermen at sea must return to nearest safe harbors and secure vessels with dual mooring lines.
3. **Storm Surge Hazard:** Storm surges exceeding 2 to 5 meters can inundate inland areas up to 10 kilometers. Evacuate immediately if your locality is designated in the storm surge hazard zone.

---

### Section 2: High-Wind Protection & Structural Securing
1. **Window & Door Reinforcement:** Fasten all windows and doors securely. Affix diagonal crisscross tape across large glass panes to minimize flying glass shards if shattered by gale-force winds.
2. **Loose Object Securing:** Remove or securely anchor all outdoor loose items including tin roofing sheets, antennas, signboards, potted plants, and construction debris which become deadly airborne missiles in winds over 100 km/h.
3. **Tree & Branch Clearance:** Trim dead overhanging tree branches away from residences and power lines before cyclone onset.

---

### Section 3: Eye of the Storm Cautionary Directive
1. **The Eye Phenomenon:** If the wind suddenly drops to total calm and the sky clears, the eye of the cyclone is passing overhead. **Do not venture outside.** The wind and destructive gusts will abruptly resume from the opposite direction with maximum violence within minutes.
2. **Stay Indoors:** Remain inside the strongest central room, hallway, or designated reinforced concrete interior space until civil defense authorities officially declare the cyclone has passed.

---

### Section 4: Power Grid, Water Storage & Communication
1. **Emergency Power & Charging:** Fully charge mobile phones, power banks, emergency flashlights, and portable radio receivers. Expect utility power shutdown prior to landfall to prevent electrocution from broken grid lines.
2. **Freshwater Stockpiling:** Fill clean overhead and household tanks, water buckets, and sealed containers with drinking water to last at least 5 days, as municipal pumps may lose power.
3. **Emergency Helplines:** Coastal Disaster Control Room **1070** / **1077**, Coast Guard Emergency **1554**, National Emergency **112**.
