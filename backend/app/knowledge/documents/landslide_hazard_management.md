# GSI & NDMA Landslide Hazard Management & Slope Safety Standard Operating Procedure (2024)
**Document ID:** GSI-NDMA-LS-2024-V2
**Source Agency:** Geological Survey of India (GSI) / National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA)
**Disaster Category:** Landslide / Mudflow / Debris Flow / Rockfall
**Applicable Environment:** Mountainous terrain, hilly slopes, cut-slope residential developments
**Last Updated:** 2024-07-02

---

### Section 1: Precursor Warning Signs & Immediate Evacuation
1. **Warning Indicators:** Be alert to early signs of slope failure: fresh cracks appearing in soil, roads, or building foundations; leaning trees, utility poles, or retaining walls; sudden seepage or bubbling of muddy water on slopes; rumbling ground vibrations.
2. **Immediate Evacuation:** Evacuate immediately if rapid debris flow or slope movement is suspected. Move uphill or laterally away from the path of the flow — never move downhill in the direction of the landslide runout zone.
3. **River/Stream Blockages:** If a mountain stream or river suddenly dries up or turns rapidly muddy, a landslide dam may have formed upstream. Move to high ground immediately to avoid sudden flash flooding if the natural dam breaches.

---

### Section 2: Safe Sheltering in Mountainous Areas
1. **Avoid Valleys & Gully Channels:** Never seek shelter in stream valleys, hollows, or natural drainage chutes during torrential rainfall.
2. **Nighttime Caution:** Landslides during night storms present extreme danger. If residing in a high-risk cut slope zone, sleep on upper floors or relocate to community shelters during heavy rainfall warnings.
3. **Emergency Numbers:** Disaster Helpline **1077**, Mountain Rescue / State Disaster Control **1070**, Emergency Response **112**.
