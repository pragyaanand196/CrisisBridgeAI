# NDMA & USGS Earthquake Response, Structural Safety & Aftershock Protocol (2024)
**Document ID:** NDMA-EQ-2024-V3
**Source Agency:** National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) / USGS Seismological Center
**Disaster Category:** Earthquake / Seismic Tremor / Structural Collapse
**Applicable Environment:** High seismic hazard zones (Zones III, IV, V), urban high-density areas
**Last Updated:** 2024-04-10

---

### Section 1: Immediate Shaking Protocol: Drop, Cover, and Hold On
1. **Drop, Cover, Hold On:** At the first sensation of tremor:
   - **DROP** down onto your hands and knees to prevent being knocked off balance.
   - **COVER** your head and neck under a sturdy table, desk, or heavy wooden furniture. If no shelter is nearby, drop next to an interior wall and cover head and neck with your arms and hands.
   - **HOLD ON** to your shelter until the shaking completely stops. Be prepared to move with the shelter if it shifts.
2. **Stay Away from Windows & Heavy Objects:** Keep clear of glass windows, display cabinets, mirrors, chimneys, unanchored bookcases, and heavy ceiling fixtures.
3. **Do Not Run Outside During Shaking:** Falling architectural masonry, exterior facade glass, and collapsing parapets make running outside the single most dangerous action during an active quake.

---

### Section 2: Evacuation After Shaking Stops & Building Safety
1. **Calm Evacuation:** Once tremors cease, evacuate buildings calmly using designated fire/emergency stairwells. **Never use elevators or lift cars**, as power failures can trap occupants in shafts.
2. **Open Ground Congregation:** Gather in designated open spaces (parks, sports grounds, broad plazas) far away from overhead electrical cables, transmission towers, tall glass buildings, and masonry walls.
3. **Structural Inspection:** Do not re-enter visibly cracked, tilted, or damaged structures until certified safe by municipal structural engineers.

---

### Section 3: Aftershock Preparedness & Gas/Electrical Safety
1. **Expect Aftershocks:** Aftershocks will occur minutes, hours, or days after the primary tremor and can cause secondary collapse of already weakened buildings. Always maintain "Drop, Cover, Hold On" readiness.
2. **Gas Leak Detection:** Check for gas leaks by smell or hissing sound. If detected, turn off the main gas valve immediately, open windows if safely accessible, and do not switch any lights or matches.
3. **Emergency Numbers:** National Emergency Response **112**, Disaster Cell **1077**, Fire Service **101**.
