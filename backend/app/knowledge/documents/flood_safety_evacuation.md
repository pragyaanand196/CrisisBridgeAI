# NDMA Official Flood Safety & Evacuation Protocol (2024 Revision)
**Document ID:** NDMA-SOP-FLD-2024-V2
**Source Agency:** National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) / Central Water Commission (CWC)
**Disaster Category:** Flood / Flash Flood / River Inundation
**Applicable Environment:** Low-lying river basins, coastal drainage zones, urban floodplains
**Last Updated:** 2024-06-15

---

### Section 1: Immediate Flood Evacuation & Movement Directives
1. **Evacuation to High Ground:** When an immediate flood warning is issued, all residents in designated low-lying sectors, riverbanks, and floodplains must immediately evacuate to elevated ground, upper floors of reinforced concrete multi-story buildings, or designated government flood relief shelters.
2. **Move Priority Groups First:** Prioritize the safe evacuation of the elderly, children, pregnant women, and persons with disabilities. Ensure household pets and livestock are untethered and moved to higher ground.
3. **Evacuation Routes:** Use only officially recommended high-ground evacuation routes. Never attempt to take unverified shortcuts through floodwaters or drainage corridors.

---

### Section 2: Moving Water & Roadway Hazards
1. **Turn Around, Don't Drown:** Never attempt to walk, swim, cycle, or drive through moving floodwaters. Just 15 centimeters (6 inches) of rapidly moving water can knock down an adult, and 30 to 60 centimeters (1 to 2 feet) of water can carry away most vehicles, including heavy SUVs.
2. **Submerged Infrastructure:** Stay away from submerged bridges, culverts, railway underpasses, and road embankments. Water pressure can cause unseen roadway collapse beneath the surface.
3. **Drainage and Manholes:** Avoid flooded streets where open manholes, storm drains, and washed-away drainage covers present fatal suction hazards.

---

### Section 3: Water Safety, Boiling, & Disease Prevention
1. **Boil Drinking Water:** Floodwaters carry severe bacterial and chemical contamination (leptospirosis, cholera, gastroenteritis, E. coli). Boil all drinking water vigorously for at least 10 minutes before consumption or use certified water purification tablets (chlorine/halazone).
2. **Food Safety:** Discard all food, raw vegetables, and unpackaged items that have come into direct contact with floodwaters. Do not use floodwater for washing utensils or oral hygiene.
3. **Water Storage:** Keep sealed containers of safe drinking water stored on elevated shelves out of flood reach.

---

### Section 4: Electrical & Structural Safety Precautions
1. **Main Power Shutoff:** Before floodwaters enter living quarters, switch off the main electrical breaker / power distribution board to eliminate electrocution hazards.
2. **Submerged Electrical Appliances:** Never touch or operate electrical switches, wall sockets, or electrical appliances while standing in water or on damp surfaces.
3. **Fallen Power Lines:** Maintain a minimum clearance of 10 meters (33 feet) from downed power lines, submerged utility poles, and broken wires. Report fallen lines immediately to the local electricity board or disaster control room.
4. **Gas Supply:** Turn off all LPG gas cylinder regulators and main gas valves before evacuating.

---

### Section 5: Essential Emergency Kit & Documentation
1. **Waterproof Documentation:** Secure all vital government identification documents (Aadhaar, passports, ration cards, property deeds, insurance policies) inside waterproof zippered pouches or heavy-duty sealed plastic bags.
2. **72-Hour Survival Kit:** Assemble an emergency go-bag containing essential prescription medications, first-aid supplies, high-calorie dry food rations, torch with extra batteries, whistle for rescue signaling, power bank, and protective footwear.
3. **Emergency Helplines:** In India, contact the National Emergency Response Number at **112**, National Disaster Helpline at **1077** / **1070**, or NDRF Control Room at **011-24363260**.
