# IMD & NDMA Severe Thunderstorm, Lightning & Cloudburst Safety Guidelines (2024)
**Document ID:** IMD-NDMA-STM-2024-V2
**Source Agency:** India Meteorological Department (IMD) / Damini Lightning Cell / NDMA
**Disaster Category:** Severe Storm / Thunderstorm / Cloudburst / Lightning Strike
**Applicable Environment:** Open fields, elevated plateaus, urban flood-prone drainage basins
**Last Updated:** 2024-05-15

---

### Section 1: Lightning 30-30 Safety Rule & Outdoor Shelter
1. **The 30-30 Rule:** If the time between seeing a lightning flash and hearing the thunder is less than 30 seconds, the lightning threat is within 10 km. Seek substantial indoor shelter immediately. Remain indoors for at least 30 minutes after hearing the last clap of thunder.
2. **Never Seek Shelter Under Isolated Trees:** Lightning naturally seeks tall, isolated elevated structures. Sheltering under a tree is one of the leading causes of outdoor lightning fatalities.
3. **Open Field Lightning Crouch:** If trapped in open fields with zero shelter options, crouch down low on the balls of your feet with heels touching, head tucked to chest, hands covering ears. Make as little contact with the ground as possible. **Never lie flat on the ground.**

---

### Section 2: Indoor Electrical & Plumbing Safety During Storms
1. **Avoid Corded Electronics & Water Pipes:** Stay away from corded telephones, electrical outlets, computers, and wired home appliances. Avoid showering, washing hands, or touching metal plumbing pipes as lightning current can travel along electrical wiring and metal pipes.
2. **Stay Away from Windows & Balconies:** Close all exterior windows, shutters, and doors. Keep clear of metal balconies and glass doors.

---

### Section 3: Cloudburst & Flash Inundation Safety
1. **Sudden Flash Floods:** Cloudbursts dump extreme rainfall (>100 mm/hour) in concentrated localized areas. Move immediately away from dry stream beds, culverts, and steep gully ravines which can fill with torrential flash floods in seconds.
2. **Emergency Numbers:** National Emergency Response **112**, Lightning Helpdesk / Disaster Cell **1077**.
