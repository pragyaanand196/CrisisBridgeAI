# NDMA & Forest Survey Wildfire Evacuation, Smoke Inhalation & Defensible Space Guidance (2024)
**Document ID:** NDMA-FSI-WLF-2024-V1
**Source Agency:** National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) / Forest Survey of India (FSI)
**Disaster Category:** Wildfire / Forest Fire / Brush Fire
**Applicable Environment:** Forest-adjacent settlements, rural foothills, arid brushlands
**Last Updated:** 2024-03-25

---

### Section 1: Wildfire Evacuation & Travel Routing
1. **Immediate Evacuation Directives:** When an evacuation order is issued for wildfire-threatened perimeters, evacuate immediately along pre-designated escape corridors. Wildfires can spread rapidly driven by shifting winds and ember storms.
2. **Vehicle Travel Precautions:** Drive with headlights on low beam, windows fully closed, air recirculation turned on, and maintain low speed through smoke.
3. **Trapped in Vehicle/Structure:** If trapped by advancing flames, stay inside the vehicle or enclosed stone/brick structure away from outer walls; lie low on the floor to avoid radiant heat and smoke.

---

### Section 2: Smoke Inhalation & Respiratory Protection
1. **N95 / P100 Respirators:** Wear tightly fitted N95 or P100 particulate respirators or damp multilayer cloth masks to filter toxic fine ash particles ($PM_{2.5}$).
2. **Indoor Air Sealing:** Keep all exterior doors, windows, and fireplace dampers closed. Seal gaps around doors with damp towels.
3. **Vulnerable Care:** Ensure children, asthma patients, elderly individuals, and people with cardiovascular conditions stay in filtered indoor environments.

---

### Section 3: Property Defensible Space & Ember Defense
1. **Clear Combustibles:** Clear dry leaves, brush, firewood stacks, and flammable debris within a 10-meter perimeter around dwellings.
2. **Water Hoses & Ladders:** Connect outdoor garden hoses and leave exterior ladders positioned against roofs for firefighting response teams.
3. **Emergency Numbers:** Fire Service **101**, Forest Control Room **1926**, National Emergency **112**.
